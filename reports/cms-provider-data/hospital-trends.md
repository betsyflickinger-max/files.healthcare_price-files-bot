- `reference/cms_provider_data/hospitals/hospital_general.parquet`: 170,709 rows, 6,045 hospitals, 32 releases (overall star rating, type, ownership)
- `reference/cms_provider_data/hospitals/hospital_measures.parquet`: 34,987,769 rows, 1,568 measures, 35 releases
- `reference/cms_provider_data/hospitals/measure_changes.csv`: each measure's first and last release and every name CMS used for it; 1,124 measures are not in the newest release (ended or renamed)

Caution for trend lines: CMS adds, drops and redefines measures, and changed how the overall star rating is worked out for the April 2021 release. Check `measure_changes.csv` before comparing across years.
